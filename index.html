Privacy Policy

Welcome to Orbital ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are firmly committed to protecting your privacy and providing a secure, transparent, and private client-side AI companion experience. This Privacy Policy outlines what information the App interacts with, how it is stored, and your rights.

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Orbital does not operate external user-tracking servers, does not collect personal identity information, and does not sell or monetize user data.

  • Local Device Storage: All chat sessions, custom provider configurations, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device's private SQLite (Room) database and encrypted preferences.
  • Encrypted API Keys: API keys you supply (e.g., Google AI Studio, Groq, OpenAI, Cerebras, Anthropic) are protected on-device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Android Keystore hardware security.
  • No Telemetry & No Advertising: Orbital contains zero third-party ads, zero tracking SDKs, and zero commercial analytics monitors.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Orbital requests specific Android permissions strictly to provide features requested directly by the user:

  • Microphone (Audio Recording): Used only when you tap the voice button or engage in voice conversation with your chosen companion mascot. Audio streams are processed in real time for speech recognition and are never stored or uploaded to secondary servers.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely to display the floating interactive avatar mascot and floating HUD chat bubble over other apps when you choose to float your companion.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain the lightweight interactive floating mascot companion and execute user-scheduled background monitoring notifications via Android's WorkManager.
  • Network Access: Used exclusively to communicate directly between your device and the AI model API provider you have configured (such as Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, or OpenAI).

3. Third-Party AI Model Providers

When you send prompts to Orbital, the text is transmitted directly via secure HTTPS to the AI provider endpoint you have selected or configured. Each provider operates under its respective privacy terms:

  • Google AI Studio Privacy Policy
  • Groq Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention & User Control

You have full, sovereign control over your data at all times:

  • Delete Chats: You can delete any chat conversation or start a fresh session at any time within the App.
  • Remove API Keys: You can clear, toggle, or replace your API keys whenever you wish.
  • Complete Purge: Clearing App Data in Android System Settings or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all stored databases and keys from your phone.
