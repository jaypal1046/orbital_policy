Block Blast Hero Privacy Policy

Developer: Jayprakash Pal · Package: com.puzzlehero.blockblast

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the current Android version of Block Blast Hero, a block puzzle game developed by Jayprakash Pal. It is separate from the privacy policy for Orbital.

2. Data collection and sharing

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include analytics, tracking, advertising-network, or payment SDKs. We do not sell user information or share it with advertisers.

3. Information stored on your device

The game stores high scores, puzzle progress, earned stars, booster counts, theme preferences, and gameplay statistics used to adjust difficulty in local app storage. These records support game features and are not sent to a developer server.

4. Advertising and purchases

The current version does not deliver third-party advertisements or accept real-money purchases. Buttons referring to rewarded ads currently grant an in-game reward without contacting an advertising provider. If future versions introduce advertising or data collection, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated.

5. Permissions and Android services

The Android app declares Internet permission for its embedded web runtime. The current game code does not use it to send gameplay records or personal information to the developer. The game does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Android backup is enabled in the app. Depending on your device and account settings, Android may back up or restore app data using the platform's backup services. Those services are controlled by your device settings and the platform provider's privacy policy.

The game stylesheet requests the Outfit typeface from Google Fonts. When fonts are downloaded, Google receives connection information such as your IP address and request headers. See Google Fonts privacy information. This request is for font delivery; game scores and progress are not included.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. You can delete local records through Android Settings > Apps > Block Blast Hero > Storage > Clear storage. This resets game progress. Any platform backups are managed through your Android or Google account settings.

7. Children and privacy

Block Blast Hero is intended for players aged 18 and older. The current game does not ask players to provide personal information and does not transmit their gameplay records to the developer.

8. Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact developer Jayprakash Pal at jayprakash.pal888@gmail.com. If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include, use it to respond to your request, and retain it only as needed to handle the conversation. Do not send sensitive information.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
